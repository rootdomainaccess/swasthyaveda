Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our online Ayurvedic consultations.

Are online Ayurvedic consultations legally valid in India?
Yes. All consultations comply fully with the Telemedicine Practice Guidelines issued by the National Medical Commission (NMC) and Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India. You receive a valid digital prescription bearing practitioner registration details.
How do I share my medical reports and lab tests?
During the booking process through our secure portal, you can upload PDFs, lab reports, X-rays, ECGs, and past prescriptions directly. The doctor reviews these prior to your video call.
How are prescribed herbal medicines delivered?
Once your digital prescription is issued, prescribed classical or proprietary Ayurvedic formulations can be dispatched directly to your doorstep through our partnered courier services across India.
How does Google Calendar integration work?
Upon successful booking confirmation, a calendar invite with the encrypted video consultation link and time details is automatically added to your Google Calendar and sent to your email.
